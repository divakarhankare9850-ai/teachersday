Dear Teacher,

Some lessons live only in a notebook. Yours live in the way we think, the questions we now ask, and the quiet confidence we carry into rooms we once found intimidating.

You noticed the effort behind the wrong answer, made room for the shy hand that finally went up, and treated our curiosity like something worth protecting. Little of that was in the syllabus — and all of it stayed with us.

Thank you for believing in us, challenging us, and helping us become a little better, one ordinary day at a time.

With gratitude, always.
